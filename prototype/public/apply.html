family_restroom Sibling Concessions

Parents enrolling 2 or more biological children receive a 10% tuition rebate on the 2nd child and 15% rebate on 3rd and subsequent children.

credit_card Flexible Payment Terms

Fees can be settled fully before term starts or split in 3 tranches (60% at registration, 20% mid-term, 20% by week 9). Instant MoMo & Bank verification supported.
